## 1. Machine Learning & Deep Learning

### [1] Vaswani et al. (2017) - Attention is All You Need
**Citation**: Vaswani, A., Shazeer, N., Parmar, N., Uszkoreit, J., Jones, L., Gomez, A. N., Kaiser, Ł., & Polosukhin, I. (2017). Attention is all you need. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems*, 30, 5998-6008.

**DOI**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762  
**Relevance**: Foundation of Transformer architecture used in ViViT

---

### [2] Arnab et al. (2021) - ViViT: A Video Vision Transformer
**Citation**: Arnab, A., Dehghani, M., Heigold, G., Sun, C., Lučić, M., & Schmid, C. (2021). ViViT: A video vision transformer. *Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision*, 6836-6846.

**DOI**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.15691  
**Relevance**: Core architecture for spatiotemporal pattern recognition in JANUS. Provides factorized encoder design.

---

### [3] Dosovitskiy et al. (2020) - An Image is Worth 16x16 Words
**Citation**: Dosovitskiy, A., Beyer, L., Kolesnikov, A., Weissenborn, D., Zhai, X., Unterthiner, T., ... & Houlsby, N. (2020). An image is worth 16x16 words: Transformers for image recognition at scale. *arXiv preprint arXiv:2010.11929*.

**DOI**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11929  
**Relevance**: Vision Transformer (ViT) foundation for patch embedding concepts

---

### [4] He et al. (2016) - Deep Residual Learning
**Citation**: He, K., Zhang, X., Ren, S., & Sun, J. (2016). Deep residual learning for image recognition. *Proceedings of the IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition*, 770-778.

**DOI**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385  
**Relevance**: ResNet architecture principles applied in deep network design

---

### [5] Ba et al. (2016) - Layer Normalization
**Citation**: Ba, J. L., Kiros, J. R., & Hinton, G. E. (2016). Layer normalization. *arXiv preprint arXiv:1607.06450*.

**DOI**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.06450  
**Relevance**: Normalization technique used in Transformer blocks

## 2. Computer Vision & Time Series

### [6] Wang & Oates (2015) - Imaging Time-Series to Improve Classification

**Relevance**: Application of GAF to deep learning classification tasks

---

### [8] Fawaz et al. (2019) - Deep Learning for Time Series Classification
**Citation**: Fawaz, H. I., Forestier, G., Weber, J., Idoumghar, L., & Muller, P. A. (2019). Deep learning for time series classification: a review. *Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery*, 33(4), 917-963.
